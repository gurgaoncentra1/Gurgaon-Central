Through the year

A rough calendar of what the city celebrates. Exact dates and venues change every year.

Oct 31

Halloween parties

Costume nights, spooky club parties and themed dinners at bars, cafés and clubs across the city on Halloween.

Nov 6–11

Diwali events

Diwali falls on Sunday, Nov 8. Expect lights, sweets, diya markets and festive crowds at Sadar Bazaar, Galleria and the malls from Dhanteras (Nov 6) to Bhai Dooj (Nov 11).

Nov – Feb

Concert season

Cooler weather means open-air gigs and live music at the city's entertainment venues.

Year round

Theatre and stage shows

Plays, comedy nights and live stage shows run at venues across the city through the year. Check the venue or ticket page for what is on.
