Yellow Line

Delhi Metro Yellow Line

5:30 AM – 11:30 PM · ₹11–₹64 · Smart card 10% off

The main link to Delhi, with stops at Sikanderpur, IFFCO Chowk, MG Road and HUDA City Centre. Tourist cards: ₹150 for 1 day, ₹500 for 3.

Rapid Metro

Rapid Metro Gurugram

About 6:05 AM – 10:45 PM · Flat ₹20 from Sikanderpur

An internal loop from Sikanderpur to Cyber City, the DLF Phases and Golf Course Road.
