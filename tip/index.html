Frequently asked questions

What are the best things to do in Gurgaon?

Gurgaon offers much more than shopping malls and corporate offices. You can explore local food markets, discover hidden historical sites, visit museums, spend time in nature parks, attend cultural events or try activities such as pottery, gaming and other creative workshops. Gurgaon Central helps you discover places and experiences across Gurugram, from popular hangouts to lesser-known local spots.

What are some hidden gems in Gurgaon that most people don't know about?

Beyond Gurgaon's malls and high-rises, you can find old stepwells, historic forts, traditional markets, quiet nature trails and neighbourhood food spots. Explore places such as Farrukhnagar Fort, Badshahpur Baoli and lesser-known corners of old Gurgaon to experience the city's history and local culture.

What are the best places to visit in Gurgaon with friends?

If you're planning a day out with friends, Gurgaon has options for different moods and budgets. Explore cafes and food markets, visit shopping destinations, try gaming or adventure activities, attend a workshop or discover a new neighbourhood. You can also plan a relaxed outing around the city's parks and cultural attractions.

What can I do in Gurgaon on the weekend?

A weekend in Gurgaon can include a cafe crawl, a pottery session, a visit to a heritage site, an outdoor walk, a food-market exploration or an evening event. Whether you're looking for a relaxed Saturday, an exciting Sunday or a full-day itinerary, Gurgaon Central helps you find ideas for your next local adventure.

Where can I find unique experiences and workshops in Gurgaon?

Gurugram has a growing range of creative and recreational experiences, including pottery workshops, art sessions, dance classes and interactive activities. Look for studios and venues offering individual sessions or group bookings if you want to try something new with friends.

Where can I find the best cafes and local food in Gurgaon?

From neighbourhood chai stalls and street-food markets to brunch cafes and specialty restaurants, Gurgaon has plenty of places to explore. Try discovering local favourites in areas such as Sector 14, Galleria Market and other food hubs across the city instead of sticking to the usual restaurant chains.

What are the best offbeat places to explore near Gurgaon?

If you want a break from the city, consider heritage attractions, nature reserves and scenic destinations around Gurugram. Places such as Sultanpur National Park, Damdama Lake and the heritage sites around Farrukhnagar offer different ways to explore the region beyond its urban centre. Check travel times, access and opening information before visiting.

Where can I find events, live music and nightlife in Gurgaon?

Gurugram has a varied social scene, with venues hosting live music, comedy shows, cultural celebrations, food events and nightlife experiences. Check current event listings and venue announcements to discover what's happening during your visit, including activities suitable for a night out with friends.

How can I plan a full day exploring Gurgaon?

Start with breakfast at a local cafe, explore a market or heritage site during the day, try a workshop or outdoor activity in the afternoon, and finish with dinner at a restaurant or food hub. Your itinerary can be tailored to your interests, budget and travel time. Gurgaon Central is a starting point for discovering places and planning your own city adventure.

Is Gurgaon and Gurugram the same city?

Yes. Gurgaon was officially renamed Gurugram in 2016, and both names are still widely used. People often search for local attractions, restaurants and activities using either name, so Gurgaon Central covers the city using both Gurgaon and Gurugram where relevant.
